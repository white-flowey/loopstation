Drum samples used by Drumloop Station

tr808/           - Roland TR-808 samples recorded by Michael Fischer, from machines.hyperreal.org,

tr707/           - Roland TR-707 samples recorded by Francois Dion, from machines.hyperreal.org,
                   distributed without licensing restrictions (via https://github.com/fluid-music/open-drums).

                   One velocity layer per piece, converted to mono WAV, trimmed and faded out (these adapted files: CC BY-SA 3.0).
